About Rally Trace

Rally Trace is a pickleball score keeper for Apple Watch and iPhone that tracks your performance game by game. Tap the side that won each rally and the watch keeps the score, the server and each player's position, and calls the score out loud. Afterward the iPhone shows how the game swung rally by rally, your heart rate and effort, your record with every partner, and whether you are improving month to month.

Scoring, records, partners, history and the Apple Health workout are free, and nothing on the watch is behind a paywall. Rally Trace Pro is an optional yearly subscription that adds serve and receive numbers and long-term trends.
